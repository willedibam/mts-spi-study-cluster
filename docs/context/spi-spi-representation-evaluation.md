## Contract and claim boundary

- The historical representation experiments used 289 SPIs from p90 and pyspi `65317c9`, not the unrestricted catalogue. Current software/catalogues have evolved; never extend these banks with a different computation version or silently reinterpret historical numbers as current-version results.
- `unified_ordered_v3` retains both ordered off-diagonal directions and yields 41,616 signed Pearson SPI-pair coordinates. Invalid values remain NaN with masks; filtering, imputation, scaling and projections fit source data only. Current implementation pointers: `src/mtsspi/spi_spi_contract.py` and `src/mtsspi/process_features.py`.
- z retains correspondence between statistics across edges, but loses edge incidence, channel locations, per-SPI offsets and positive scales. Common channel/edge permutations and simultaneous MPI transposition leave it unchanged. Subsampling, channel mixing and changing duration have no general invariance guarantee. Functional dependence sensitivity is not unique causal-mechanism identification.
- Deterministic z=f(X) cannot contain raw-inaccessible information. The supported claim concerns efficient extraction by specified learners under label/observation constraints. Most positive evidence is for fixed z plus learned PCA/PLS/readouts; cross-task reuse of one learned embedding is untested.
- Valid Pearson/Spearman SPI correlation blocks are PSD Gram matrices, rank at most min(K,M(M−1)−1), generally not SPD. Euclidean z distance corresponds to Frobenius distance up to sqrt(2). MI similarity need not be PSD. No advantage from a new matrix-geometry learner is established.
- Cliff et al. (2023) already compared record-level SPI matrix correlations before averaging across datasets; novelty must be useful record-level representation behaviour, not correlation between statistics itself. Variable-channel neural learning, HCGA and bilinear pooling are relevant precedents. See [study design](../../research/representation/representation_state/notes/spi-representation-learning-study.md).
- User rejects universal neural-superiority claims, growing benchmark grids and changing generators merely to force wins. Retain failures and strong task-informed controls. Paper splitting and venue are undecided; group membership and earlier enthusiasm are not evidence of novelty. Pearson’s favourable bias–variance tradeoff remains a hypothesis, not an established consequence of compression.

## How the hypothesis developed

- Broad 14-class gains were concentrated: 76.4% of the net gain came from two Gaussian VAR classes, and a simple VAR specialist solved the diagnostic. Future Kuramoto coherence also favoured simpler physical/marginal controls; PCA sweeps did not rescue z. These were useful limits, not broad nonlinear-learning successes. See the [evidence map](../../research/representation/representation_state/notes/spi-representation-evidence-map.md) and [earlier exclusions](spi-spi-representation-evaluation-ruled-out.md).
- [Interaction-share confirmation](../../research/representation/interaction_share/notes/interaction-share-findings.md): 40-label shifted MAE z-PLS .1511 versus normalized SPI distributions .1853, but a subsequently added ordinary autospectral control achieved .1075. Realizable phase controls showed correspondence mattered to z, not that it was necessary for best prediction. The omitted spectral comparator was a material correction.
- [Covariance modulation](../../research/representation/covariance_modulation/notes/covariance-modulation-findings.md): stationary Gaussian mixtures matched individual-channel laws and population covariance/cross-spectra while changing conditional dependence; 440 masters/640 views. Reduced 40-label MAE z-PCA .2258 and z-PLS .2375 were near median .2265; corrected pointwise neural .1652 and a model-informed observed-data moment reference .1261 were better.
- A real clipped-validation plateau was fixed before interpreting the neural comparison; one bounded extension to 1,200 epochs left no selected folds at the ceiling and preserved the negative z finding. PCA/PLS were already used. Do not explain failure simply as insufficient regularization or neural undertraining.
- Exact two-statistic example: cross-cumulant K scales with alpha² while Pearson corr(R,K) remains invariant for positive alpha. This illustrates loss of target-bearing magnitude, not invariance/uninformativeness of all 289-SPI z. MI/dcorr summaries also contained some signal, with substantial observation-shift failures.
- The refined hypothesis targeted co-organization of distinct dependence modes. This followed negative results; it was not the untouched day-one hypothesis. A subsequent fresh pilot and transfer tests were frozen prospectively. Phase versus envelope coupling provides physical motivation, but the synthetic binary partition is not an established anaesthesia biomarker.

## Co-organization: completed central positive evidence

- [Scout](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-coorganization-scout.md): four phase groups and four envelope groups, each of eight channels; partitions coincide or cross. Each mode separately has an isomorphic latent co-membership graph, and single-channel process laws match in distribution. Finite-sample spectra and all catalogue-SPI distributions need not match. Labels come from latent organization, never estimated z; common drives are not direct causal edges.
- [Fresh pilot](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-coorganization-pilot.md), frozen `a35f632`: 320 independent masters, three disjoint 40-record source pools and 200 independent test masters. Nested 10/20/40 total labels; M16/T1000 source and nested M8/T500 target, fixed latent N32, no pretraining/augmentation. The earlier 96-view paired scout remains exploratory and separate.
- [Verified results](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-coorganization-findings.md): reduced BA at 10/20/40 labels z-PLS .8883/.960/.980, z-PCA .830/.960/.985, rich SPI summaries .6433/.740/.8083. At 40 labels, z-PLS minus summaries is +17.17 points, conditional pointwise 95% CI [13.50,21.17]; AUROC .9986 versus .9215. At full size/40 labels z=1.0 and summaries=.9883, so the main separation is under constraints/transfer.
- The direct observed phase/envelope specialist obtains .9633/.9633/.980 reduced BA and is stronger at ten labels. No latent labels/groups are supplied. Rich summaries are 23 descriptors per SPI, not complete MPIs; complete MPIs contain z. The results do not prove that all useful information is absent from all marginal learners.
- [Correspondence intervention](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-coorganization-mechanism.md): independently permuting dyads per SPI preserves complete edge multisets, reciprocity and validity, but retrained z drops to approximately chance; common permutations preserve z. This is a representation intervention, not a realizable physiological surrogate. Removing envelope-SPIs weakens but does not eliminate prediction; a 36-coordinate PEC×PLV panel remains strong. Fixed nonlinear marginal controls did not close the gap, but do not exhaust nonlinear learners.
- [Learned pooling](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-pooling-transfer.md): 21,697-parameter shared edge MLP and mean/SD aggregation retains SPI identities and validity, without topology. Reduced BA .6583/.965/.9883 at 10/20/40 labels. Two selected ten-label folds hit the 600-epoch ceiling, limiting an optimization-independent small-data claim. It receives more distribution/validity information than z, so aggregation flexibility is not the only differing factor.
- [Faster-regime transfer](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-transfer-findings.md): 200 fresh targets, frozen source models; reduced 40-label BA z-PCA .990, z-PLS .9917, pooling .995, summaries .7933. Carrier, diffusion and envelope timescales shift; faster is not established to be harder.
- [Direct-mechanism transfer](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-mechanism-transfer-findings.md): 200 fresh targets replace shared phase drivers with noisy Kuramoto coupling, retaining envelope/observation laws and matching group diffusion. Physical, step-size and coupling-removal checks precede evaluation. Reduced 40-label BA z-PCA .890, z-PLS .8883, pooling .9383, summaries .675; at full size z/pooling=1.0 versus summaries=.7183. This is controlled mechanism transfer, not arbitrary cross-system generalization.
- Direct-mechanism reduced AUROC remains approximately .998 for z/pooling. A post-hoc top-half rule using known 50% prevalence improves decisions, but uses extra target-batch information and is not primary source-only performance. Ranking retention and threshold transfer must remain separate. Ninety-nine frozen source references and feature/model replays are verified in the linked report.
- [Pooling attribution](../../research/representation/oscillatory/notes/oscillatory-pooling-attribution.md) separately retrains three correspondence nulls: intact reduced BA .9883 versus .4917/.5117/.5033. Both fixed and learned aggregation exploit correspondence; Pearson optimality is not established. These synthetic phases are complete; no saturated repeat or new generator is justified merely by available compute.

## Neural/library comparisons and NeuroTycho

- [InceptionTime](../../research/representation/learned_baselines/notes/inceptiontime-followup.md) is complete: 55 final members, five-member ensembles, original six-module architecture and declared source-only tuning adaptation. At full M16/T1000 and 40 labels, all three synthetic targets have ensemble BA .50333 versus z=1.0. Four selected folds reach 1,500 epochs only at ten labels; source validation remains poor at 20/40. This establishes tested finite-data failure, not representational impossibility.
- [MiniRocket](../../research/representation/learned_baselines/notes/minirocket-synthetic-followup.md): native aeon 1.5 multivariate transform/ridge, 27 fits, same full-size tasks/cohorts. At 40 labels, original/faster/direct BA .52722/.500/.46889. Native ridge selection reaches grid endpoints; do not imply optimal tuning or all-Rocket failure. MultiRocket remains untested; tsfresh/catch22 were not evaluated on this synthetic core.
- [NeuroTycho](../../research/representation/neurotycho/notes/neurotycho-transfer-findings.md): awake-eyes-closed versus sustained anaesthesia, KTMD sources across 11 dates/four macaques, each target animal excluded from its fit; propofol targets are four dates/two animals. Full M16/T2000 and reduced M8/T1000 share windows/filter context. Source-only grouped selection, fixed threshold, no target calibration; drug/calendar period are confounded and target animals appeared in exploratory development.
- Full/reduced BA: z-PCA .9297/.9062, z-PLS .9453/.9141, rich summaries .8359/.6641, conventional spectra .8672/.9531, matched raw encoder .8307/.8724, enriched raw encoder .9635/.9740, learned SPI pooling .8307/.7812. Spectra are ordinary within-channel power summaries, not spectral SPIs; their AUROC=1.0 means interaction necessity is unestablished. z can still have practical readout/threshold advantages without a unique physiological explanation.
- Enriched raw uses the same architecture with more eligible source windows and channel/duration augmentation, not extra labelled animals/dates. It is a legitimate practical comparator; more windows and augmentation are not separately identified. Matched seeds vary substantially. No demonstrated low-subject label-efficiency curve, cross-task reuse, onset detection or clinical validation.
- Matched-input InceptionTime ensemble BA .953125 versus z-PLS .945313 and z-PCA .929688; do not substitute its weaker individual-member average. Two target animals cannot establish precise population rankings. [Library follow-up](../../research/representation/neurotycho/notes/neurotycho-library-followup-findings.md): full BA MiniRocket .89844, tsfresh .84375, catch22 .83594, with near-perfect ranking. These pipelines do not represent every method in each library.
- NeuroTycho verification checks 4,096 prediction rows and all 32 source fits; InceptionTime checks 174 metric rows/200 CPU replays; MiniRocket checks 81 rows/27 fits. Relevant numerical repairs (float32 ties, CPU/CUDA differences, float32 BLAS batch effects) and hashes remain in the linked reports. Do not rerun verified experiments without a new substantive reason.

## Research assessment and open scientific question

The strongest supported learning claim is that cross-statistic correspondence is a useful, selective inductive bias for transferable inference of constructed interaction organization, with qualified empirical state-transfer feasibility. It is not inaccessible raw information, universal neural dominance, a proven optimal Pearson compression, or an identified mechanism of consciousness. A meaningful methods contribution is supported; a standalone Nature Computational Science-level advance is not established merely by the present benchmark wins. The next decisive uncertainty is whether this representation enables consequential scientific inference, or a more general predictive/mechanistic principle, beyond the deliberately aligned synthetic task. No larger run is justified solely to reverse an unfavourable ranking. Existing case-study illustrations and the separate corpus/order-coordinate programme need their own evidential assessment.
