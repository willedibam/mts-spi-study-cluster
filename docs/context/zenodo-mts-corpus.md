- Clusters: Ward on the full-rank geometry, k = 40, fitted once and only drawn on maps. Hennig cluster-wise Jaccard over 50 subsets keeping 80% of 688 parent keys: median .80 with 25 groups ≥ .75, against median .63 for K-means k = 40. Global separation is weak (Ward-40 silhouette .10), so the map is mostly a continuum with a minority of compact groups.

  - Size-matched null: every candidate exceeds all 1,000 same-size sets drawn from other sources, matched per member on (log M, log T). A (.51) and C (.30) are lower because their matched pools are themselves cohesive: FingerMovements (28 × 50) forms its own equally distinct cluster next to A, and the generator grid that matches C is not drawn toward C.

Removing the within-parent estimate changes no candidate silhouette by more than .02, and Ward-40 refits recover every candidate at or above a shuffled-size placebo. Under the pooled over-control F falls below placebo (Jaccard .32 vs .52) and E sits at it (.71 vs .69). The within-parent surface is identified only on synthetic generators at T ≥ 100.
  - Same parent at several sizes (synthetic only): C's lattice core keeps all 14 sizes and G 22 of 23; E loses geometric Brownian motion at T = 100 and one LEGION configuration at M = 5. Within real sources membership does not follow size (Cricket at one size spans five clusters; PEMS-SF's E split vs M has AUC .41).
  - A's neighbourhood is shared with real multichannel recordings in general (real outsiders with M ≥ 10 rank A 3rd-nearest; the synthetic grid ranks it 11th–12th), not specifically with T ≈ 2M; no single SPI category carries that adjacency.
- The size effect that matters is precision. z's sampling noise falls with M: the 5-NN distance is 43 at M ≤ 6 and 21 at M ≥ 17 (Spearman −.83, the same within the generator grid). Raw silhouettes therefore favour large-M groups, so state separation relative to size-matched sets. Disclose A's single size and E's size-selected synthetic members, and keep F as a region.
