## Strength as a per-recording nuisance on proof classes, 2026-10-06

Question from the user: can coupling strength be normalised per MTS so that mean |r| and per-SPI means become noise while z keeps structure. Run `proof-strength-nuisance-261006` (full p90, M16/T1000): eight coupled classes (ring VARs .2/.39 and .7/.14 at radius .98, Kuramoto K=3.5, wave, CML frozen-chaos/sti-i/defect-turbulence/fdstc) plus unmodulated Gaussian/Cauchy noise, 40 realizations, each observed in five arms through homogeneous white sensor noise: native; equalised (mean |r|=.14); mild (η~U[.3,.9]); matched (mean |r|~U[.08,.20], same in every class); wide (attenuation U[.25,.95]). Fresh-seed repeat of native/matched at M24. 2,400 records, zero failures. Executed [notebook](../../notebooks/embeddings/proof_strength_nuisance_261006.ipynb); code `scripts/{proof_strength_nuisance,build_proof_strength_notebook}.py`; unsupervised fits per arm and panel with the proof preprocessing.

- **Equalising fails again; varying works partly.** Equalised strength leaves standardized means as clustered as native (CML silhouette .44 vs .43; across classes means .50 beat z .41). Strength that varies between recordings spreads the means along it: CML silhouette of means .26 (matched) and .13 (wide) against z .52 and .51; M24 matched .32 against .61. The mean UMAP then loses separate CML clusters and z keeps four. The CML ordering survives interquartile and rank-Gaussian scaling of the means (wide: .14, .24), unit-variance z (.34) and four UMAP seeds. This is the closest result yet to the requested picture, and it is a geometry result only.
- **Means are never noise.** Five-neighbour class agreement and grouped logistic accuracy stay ≥.98 for means and z in every arm and both panels; dropping the mean vector's PC1 does not restore compactness (.12). Across families the mean UMAP still separates classes. Mean |r| alone is at chance when matched (.31; chance .25).
- **z is less strength-sensitive, not blind.** Within a class (wide arm), 80% of SPI means have |Spearman| with mean |r| above .5 against 26% of z coordinates (medians .73 vs .40). Under heavy noise the two VARs and Kuramoto merge in two dimensions for both representations.
- **Qualifications.** Within CML z is already more compact natively (gap .21; .25 matched; .38 wide; M24 .25 to .28), so only the growth is attributable to strength. Sensor noise only attenuates: fdstc (native mean |r| .13 at M16, .10 at M24) is left as generated in 55%/78% of matched recordings. Equalised and matched put more noise on natively stronger classes; mild and wide do not. One nuisance, one length.
- **Design lessons.** Exact matching needs native strength above the target range; ring systems dilute mean |r| with M; a 13-statistic local probe run agreed with p90 in direction and was not used as evidence. Kuramoto at K=-4 (the proof setting, native .20) is too weak for these targets, hence K=3.5.
- Identity worth stating in any write-up: standardising each SPI's profile within a recording zeroes every mean and leaves z exactly unchanged, so z is by construction what remains after removing the set of strengths.

## Dial network pilot, 2026-10-07

Classes that differ only in how channels interact. Identical AR(1) nodes on a random acyclic graph (at most two inputs), x_i(t)=a x_i(t-1)+k g_i Σ_j W_ij φ_β(x_j(t-τ_ij)/σ_a)+ε; φ_β mixes bounded odd (tanh) and even (Gaussian bump) terms. Six classes = shape (β 0 or 1) × lag (1; 5; U{1..9} per edge) plus uncoupled, 40 each; gain k log-uniform [.25,1.2] and a~U[.3,.7] per recording as nuisances; sweeps of β and of lag spread, 24 per level. M16/T1000, 496 records, full p90, none failed (two reran after a walltime kill; jobs 180712034/039/045, 180720164). Executed [notebook](../../notebooks/embeddings/dial_network_261007.ipynb); code `scripts/{dial_network,build_dial_network_notebook}.py`; remote `proof/dial-network-261007`.

- **Closest result to the requested picture.** Five-neighbour class agreement: mean |r| .24, means .46, z .73 (chance .14); ten-PC logistic .34/.66/.84; shape dial .72 vs .88, lag dial .57 vs .79. Mean embedding is a gain-ordered fan with classes interleaved; z has the uncoupled class at a tip and classes along branches. Diffuse, not clustered (z silhouette .07).
- Both need detectable coupling: weakest gain third .28 (means) vs .53 (z); strongest .62 vs .85.
- **z is not blind to gain or timescale**: within-class variance shares gain 36%, a 18% (means 46%, 16%); class 27% in both. Gain moves z along a class's branch, and means along a direction common to all classes.
- **Dose-response fails.** No leading component of means or z follows β or lag spread more than gain; the pre-named z(covariance, Kraskov MI) follows gain (.75) not β (.39), because receivers of a shared driver correlate linearly even under even coupling; lag spread leaves no trace (|ρ|≤.19). The "how nonlinear, how variably lagged" claim has no support from this system yet.
- Record only, not in the notebook: the strength-normalised mean profile reaches .69 class agreement here.
- Scout lessons: feedback loops with bounded coupling gave multistability and vanishing dependence at high gain, hence the acyclic graph; even coupling is not invisible to correlation at network level (median mean |r| .05–.13).
