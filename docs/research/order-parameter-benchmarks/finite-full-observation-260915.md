## Bounded physics pilot, not yet a headline candidate: chaotic phase synchronization

Use the original detuned Rössler pair of [Rosenblum, Pikovsky and Kurths, PRL 76, 1804 (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.76.1804), not the different parameters of their later lag-synchronization study. For i=1,2 and j≠i:

\[
\dot x_i=-\omega_i y_i-z_i+C(x_j-x_i),\qquad
\dot y_i=\omega_i x_i+0.15y_i,\qquad
\dot z_i=0.2+z_i(x_i-10),\quad \omega_{1,2}=1\pm0.015.
\]

The established diagnostic is the mean angular-frequency mismatch Q=|Ω1−Ω2|, corroborated by phase-slip/winding statistics. The published control plane is coupling C versus intrinsic frequency mismatch Δω: an entrainment tongue separates drifting from phase-locked behavior. We take the fixed Δω=.015 intercept, with original illustrative couplings .01, .027 and .035. This is a finite-dimensional dynamical synchronization transition, not a thermodynamic order parameter or an analytic formula for Q(C). Amplitudes can remain chaotic and nonidentical when phases lock; this is precisely the useful distinction from identical-signal synchronization. All six physical coordinates are recorded.

Near-zero measured frequency mismatch does not prove indefinitely bounded phase difference. [Pazó, Zaks and Kurths (2003)](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/members/kurths/publikationen/2003/200_Ch13_role_of_unstable.pdf) found extremely rare slips and revised apparent thresholds for a related parameter cut. Do not transfer their numerical threshold to this different model, or label C≈.028 as exact. Report the measurement horizon and finite-run boundary bracket. Circular phase coherence is secondary, not an interchangeable definition of the locking boundary.
