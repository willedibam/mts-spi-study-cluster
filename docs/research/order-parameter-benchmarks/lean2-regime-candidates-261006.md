## Physics and initial scout (before p90)

**CGLE:** $A_t=(1+ic_1)A_{xx}+A-(1-ic_3)|A|^2A$, periodic domain, $c_1=3.5$. Target phase turbulence → defect turbulence, not the Benjamin–Feir line $c_3=1/c_1$. [Torcini, Frauenkron & Grassberger](https://arxiv.org/html/chao-dyn/9608003), especially section III, identifies space-time defect density as a genuine order parameter. Published estimates of the transition around $c_3\sim0.7$–$0.77$ depend strongly on rare-event resolution; do not present an exact analytic boundary. Initial scout: $c_3=0.6,0.7,0.75,0.8,0.9,1.0$, lengths 128 and 512, spacing 0.5, time step 0.025, four seeds each. Start from the zero-winding uniform oscillation with independent uniform ±0.1 real/imaginary perturbations. Burn 2,000 time units, observe 1,000 samples at spacing 0.5 from 16 evenly dispersed sites, then estimate Q on a disjoint 4,000-time-unit full-field window. N denotes complex grid sites, not a claim of 16-dimensional full observation. Local space-time plaquette winding counts both signs of defects even when global winding changes cancel. Exact local flow and spectral diffusion use Strang splitting; validate plane-wave convergence, event counting, then time/grid refinement if physical behavior qualifies.

**Driven rate network:** $dx_i=[-x_i+\sum_jJ_{ij}\tanh x_j]dt+\sqrt{2}\sigma dW_i$, independent neuron inputs, $J_{ij}\sim\mathcal N(0,g^2/N)$ and $J_{ii}=0$. [Schuecker, Goedeke & Helias, PRX 8, 041029](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.01880) studies the reliable/chaotic boundary. Q is the largest conditional Lyapunov exponent, not a thermodynamic order parameter. The tangent uses the same input realization as the trajectory; tangent/replica channels never enter the observed MTS. At $\sigma^2=0.125$, the published large-N boundary is near $g=1.48$; this is not assumed for N=16. Initial scout: $g=1.1,1.3,1.45,1.6,1.8,2.0$, N=16,64,256, four connectivity seeds per size, M=16. The quenched unit connectivity is held across g within seed; noise is independent per control. Burn 1,000, record T=1,000 at spacing 0.5, future Lyapunov window 3,000. Additive-noise Heun and its corresponding tangent map use dt=0.02. Independent analytic OU variance and tangent checks at g=0 precede submission. Grid/time refinement and larger-N checks remain necessary before interpreting critical positions.

Larger size is justified only by a measurable finite-size limitation, not compute availability.
