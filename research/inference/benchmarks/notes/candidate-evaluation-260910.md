# Candidate evaluation against the original objective

## Decision

2026-10-06 scope distinction: the completed portfolio below addresses generic order-coordinate recovery. The user's new search specifically requires new candidates where centered SPI–SPI PC1 first clearly outperforms mean absolute Pearson, then is tested against mean-SPI PC1. Re-listing existing positive benchmarks does not answer this stronger question. The literature shortlist below is a search result, not a simulation result or an authorized production plan.

### New search: dependence-type transitions beyond average strength

**First priority: higher-order/cross-frequency locking.** [Komarov & Pikovsky (2015), PRE92,012906](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.06193) derives a native two-population 2:1 resonant phase model, generalized harmonic order parameters, stationary entrained branches and collective-chaos regimes. The mechanistic reason to screen it is that cos(theta) and cos(2theta) have zero population Pearson correlation despite exact nonlinear dependence; taking absolute Pearson does not repair this. This identity is illustrative, not proof that the whole population's mean absolute correlation stays flat. Prefer a cross-community locking boundary with already coherent communities over their initial incoherent-to-coherent onset, which may be easy for average correlation. Published theory is thermodynamic; N=16/32 fully observed populations are proposed finite-size pilots, not validated sizes. Retain natural frequency dispersion and audit exact duplicates/rank/constant SPI profiles. No artificial padding or independent replicas merely to inflate M.

A minimal analytic physics check is the resonant Adler reduction, dot(theta)=omega, dot(psi)=2omega+Delta-kappa*sin(psi-2theta), hence dot(phi)=Delta-kappa*sin(phi), phi=psi-2theta. Its locking boundary is kappa=abs(Delta), and the deterministic phase-slip rate is sqrt(Delta^2-kappa^2) outside locking, zero inside (kappa>=0). Higher-order locking is established; [an experimental optomechanical example](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25884-x) observes 2:1,3:1,4:1 Arnold tongues. A harmonic phase-coherence statistic and mean-frequency mismatch are defensible Q candidates, but cannot both be claimed to be identical to a particular population paper's order parameter without checking the exact cut. The normal form is an analytic mechanism check, not itself a demonstrated M>=16 benchmark. The key second-gate risk is that nonlinear MI/distance-correlation means may already recover the change. No first-order jump or SPI–SPI success is asserted.

**Second priority, physical but harder: one-dimensional complex Ginzburg–Landau phase-to-defect turbulence.** [Torcini, Frauenkron & Grassberger, Secs.I/III](https://arxiv.org/html/chao-dyn/9608003) explicitly identifies defect density as an order parameter, unlike correlation lengths and Kaplan–Yorke dimension which need not be singular at the boundary. The PDE is A_t=(1+i*c1)A_xx+A-(1-i*c3)|A|^2*A. Both regimes are chaotic, but defect turbulence adds amplitude zeros/phase slips and destroys winding conservation: a genuine change of dynamical organization. Use event density from independently resolved full-field spacetime, not an arbitrary near-zero threshold or net winding change that can miss cancelling events. The paper's alternative maximal sustainable phase gradient requires many initial winding sectors, not one ordinary recording. At c1=3.5, published estimates for the transition differ (~.70–.77 depending on method/statistics); the very low defect rate and long observation times make this a risky short-T benchmark, not an exact analytic threshold. Its simulations use physical lengths1024–4096 and grid spacing.5; a large-field/subsampled-M construction is more plausible than M=N16. Correlation's likely weakness and z's possible sensitivity are hypotheses. Do not transfer the one-dimensional claim to two dimensions, where [Manneville & Chaté (1996)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0167278996000450) discuss thermodynamic metastability.

**Reserve: noise-driven random rate networks, reliable-to-chaotic response.** [Schuecker, Goedeke & Helias (2018), PRX8,041029](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.8.041029) gives a coupling/input-amplitude phase diagram and largest conditional Lyapunov exponent. Both sides fluctuate under the native external input; auxiliary replicas with identical input are diagnostic machinery, never observed channels. This is a meaningful stability quantity rather than a thermodynamic order parameter. The source's population validation uses N5000; finite N16/32 phase behavior and absolute-correlation weakness are unverified. Mean-field single-unit effective Gaussian statistics and visible temporal changes mean z may contain no useful extra information; temporal SPI means are credible competitors. Lower priority than resonance for this request, especially because a sharp order-parameter jump is not guaranteed.

**Do not promote superficially attractive alternatives:** explosive/inertial synchronization merely for a sharper coherence jump (mean absolute correlation can rise too); in-phase/antiphase cluster switching (absolute Pearson may be blind but signed means can expose it); amplitude death/rest-to-chaos (constant-channel side); an arbitrary switch splicing two generators (manufactured sharpness); or a pure higher-order interaction with unchanged distributions of every measured MPI profile (no reason z must recover it). The classic [phase-to-lag synchronization transition](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.4193) is distinct from the completed phase-entrainment test, but lagged-correlation/MI means make it a lower-priority second-gate candidate, not an assumed solution.

Screen on development simulations only: independently verify Q/boundary and finite observation validity, then require a clear q advantage over mean absolute Pearson before examining mean-SPI PC1 and launching fresh held validation. Q not being algebraically a mean correlation is insufficient: both can track the same control. A change in dependence type does not guarantee a changed z, nor target-blind PC1 dominance. Conversely, failure of mean-SPI PC1 does not establish absence of information in the full mean vector. No new computation or reminder was launched in this search.

## Requirements retained

- Recognizable model with an established independently defined physical order parameter and a studied control-parameter boundary. A local intercept suffices.
- Clear finite-run order contrast; a sharp continuous transition or dynamical bifurcation qualifies. A first-order discontinuity is not mandatory.
- Genuine nondegenerate multivariate dynamics on both sides, without modifying the model merely to rescue the representation.
- M=N at small size is ideal, not mandatory. Large physical N and M<=32 partial observations are legitimate, with finite-size/time and sampling qualifications.
- Target/control-blind representation fitting, followed by validation against Q. Inference of a latent order-related coordinate is legitimate terminology; numerical calibration, equation discovery and instantaneous state recovery are separate, stronger claims.
- Variable M,T comparability is the methodological motivation; superiority to purpose-built statistics and direct clinical interpretation are not required.
- Preserve prior Ising-family implementation exclusion. Literature assessment does not authorize reinstating those simulations.

### Precisely specified CML alternative

Marcq–Chaté–Manneville use the 2D nearest-neighbor synchronous lattice with f(x)=r*x*(1-x). At g=.2, the collective period-1/period-2 boundary is r_c=3.86212(12). With xbar(t) the spatial mean, the published order parameter is Q=<|xbar(2t+1)-xbar(2t)|>. Individual sites remain chaotic on both sides. The critical study uses L32–128 (N1024–16384); its illustrative bifurcation diagram uses L1024. Thus a million-site run is precedent, not a minimum. This is a defensible optional across-boundary example, not evidence of fixed-control switching or small-M SPI recovery. Preserve synchronous updates and unit-step sampling; an even stride can erase the period-two distinction.

## Selection principles

### Focused follow-ups after finite-system confirmation

Keep the primary positive portfolio; stop pursuing the current smallCML, L96 and HR arms as positive exhibits, while retaining concise negative results. Do not homogenize all existing control grids: common counts are not common physical resolution. For a future selected benchmark, roughly21 controls plus a physics-justified denser local bracket is a useful default, not a mandate; use32 independent confirmation seed clusters when precision is needed. Preserve original grids/roles rather than retrospectively recasting them. Existing Rössler21, TASEP21, fineSL19 and confirmedCML17 grids need no cosmetic rerun. Add seeds to narrow uncertainty; add control points to localize a boundary. Both must follow physical time/burn-in convergence, and neither alone creates a sharp finite-system transition.

The highest-value completed no-extraction follow-up is the unchanged N32 TASEP model applied to N64 cached features. All168rows pass, held84rho=.782 [.726,.867]; all source model arrays/sign/scale are identical. This is retrospective empirical size transfer (N and M change together), not fresh confirmation or arbitrary-size invariance. Evidence and reproduction are in the finite-full-observation report and notebook. Additional paired-T work is optional because SL/CML already address variable observation sizes; no blanket T or larger-M bank was launched.

Resource scoping at T1000/full289 p90: matched TASEP median times are18.09min atM32 and60.39min atM64 (168datasets each); N32fresh confirmation median18.98min (672datasets). Pair-count planning, t(M)=60.39min×M(M−1)/(64×63), gives approximately2.47h/M100,9.93h/M200,16.30h/M256,65.31h/M512. These are unvalidated extrapolations, not predicted runtimes or upper bounds. Two measured sizes imply a descriptive exponent1.74 but cannot establish asymptotic scaling. Larger M may affect covariance/model-estimator validity at fixedT, not just cost. Peak single-worker memory near24.4GiB atM64 and some joint-estimator workspaces prevent a trustworthy RAM extrapolation from MPI-array size. Before a scientifically justified large-M bank, run a small representative timing/memory/validity scout; do not treat available SU as sufficient evidence of feasibility. No resource-only farm or reminder is active.

Primary sources: [Chua crisis and characteristic-time scaling, PRE52,2268(1995)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.52.2268); [Manneville–Pomeau Lorenz intermittency, PhysicaD1,219–226(1980)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(80)90013-5), with [original1979 report](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(79)90255-X); [Tanaka–Lichtenberg–Oishi inertial transition, PRL78,2104(1997)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.2104) and [finite-size study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.3724). These are literature-grounded candidates, not reproduced protocols or positive results. Avoid building an ensemble of independent copies or adding drive/duplicate channels simply to inflate M. Hénon–Heiles escape has a clear energy threshold but escaping/nonstationary records make it a less clean fit; Josephson voltage steps are physically compelling but a suitable nondegenerate small-array observation protocol is not yet established here. Neither outranks the first two for this task.

Sharpness, representational recoverability, and informativeness of a particular small observation are separate questions. No literature result guarantees z recovery from arbitrary M32 subsets. Dispersed sites are a reasonable default for spatially representative coverage; contiguous patches test local sensing. Neither universally wins. A larger N sharpens some asymptotic contrasts while increasing mixing time or reducing the representativeness of a fixed patch.

First-order systems are not automatically cleaner: branch choice, nucleation, hysteresis and measurement horizon can complicate their Q. Conversely, a continuous transition is not a weak benchmark merely because it lacks a jump. For this proof of concept, preserve the existing three distinct physical mechanisms plus Kuramoto; add at most one system only for a specific missing capability, not to maximize count or choose the most flattering result.
