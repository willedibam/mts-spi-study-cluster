1,053 recordings placed by their SPI–SPI agreement profile z. Groups are density cores found in feature space (HDBSCAN on the first 50 principal components), not on the map. Tier 1 groups are stable under resampling by parent recording, span at least three parent recordings, and appear as a single region on the displayed map; only they are shaded by default. Tier 2 pass the first two tests but split on the map. Tier 3 are the remaining density groups. Recordings in no group are drawn small and faint. Co-location means a shared dependence-organisation signature, not a shared mechanism.
