MTS corpus atlas / v4 · map regions and density groups

1,053 recordings placed by their SPI–SPI agreement profile z. Two kinds of unit. Map regions (R) are found on the chosen 2-D map, then checked in feature space (the first 50 principal components): tighter than random and size-matched sets, and stable when the map is refitted on subsets of recordings. Candidates, shaded by default, are checked regions that hold at least two sources with two or more parent recordings each; they are the current basis for picking examples. Density groups (G) are found in feature space directly; tier 1 groups are stable, span at least three parent recordings and appear as one region on the displayed map, and tier 2 split on the map. Recordings in no density group are drawn small and faint. Co-location means a shared dependence-organisation signature, not a shared mechanism.
