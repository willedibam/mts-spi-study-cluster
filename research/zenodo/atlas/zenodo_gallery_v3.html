MTS corpus atlas / v3 · one t-SNE map, feature-space clusters

1,053 recordings placed by their SPI–SPI agreement profile z (289 statistics, all M(M−1) ordered entries). Clusters are Ward groups fitted in the full feature space and only drawn here. Co-location means a shared dependence-organisation signature, not a shared mechanism. Gaps and blob sizes on a t-SNE map are partly set by perplexity and exaggeration.

Dark grey = real, light grey = synthetic, hollow = unlabelled in the archive. Dot area grows with process count M on a fixed scale. Shaded regions are approximate 80%/50% KDE mass contours of a feature-space cluster's projected points: display guides, not boundaries or confidence regions. Scroll to zoom; drag to pan.
