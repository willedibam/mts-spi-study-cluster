# SPI marginal and strength baselines

The question is whether SPI–SPI correlations across aligned channel pairs describe dependence character beyond simpler summaries: mean covariance/Pearson correlation, one mean per MPI ($m$), and seven distribution summaries per MPI. Diverse systems are central; small constructed examples provide supporting intuition. Raw MPI means can themselves encode character, and a mixed two-dimensional embedding does not imply that a representation contains little information.

**Current conclusion:** matching a scalar strength does not generally erase marginal class information. SPI–SPI improves some retrieval and geometry comparisons, but the strong diverse-family demonstration with weak raw p90 means remains unestablished. Later user direction accepts means that are markedly more diffuse than z, rather than requiring chance performance. Results below are exploratory unless the linked study explicitly identifies a frozen confirmation.
